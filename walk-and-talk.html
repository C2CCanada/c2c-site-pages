How it works

  1. Email us to ask for a spot with Colin or Nicole. Tell us your name, the best number to reach you, what you’d like to talk about, and the days or times that suit you.

  2. We reply with a time and our e-transfer details. Spots go in the order we receive emails.

  3. Send a $50 Interac e-Transfer to hold your spot. Once it comes through, the time is yours.

  4. Pick up the phone. Colin or Nicole calls you at the agreed time and the walk begins.

Why a walk?

Because it’s how we think best. No slides, no screen share, no sales pitch. Just a real conversation with one of the people who run the business, in the fresh air, on their own two feet.

Walk and Talk is a conversation about how we do things. It isn’t legal, tax or investment advice, and it isn’t an offer to invest. For advice on your own situation, speak with a qualified professional.
