Digital Employees

Let’s hire your first digital employee.

A digital employee is an AI staff member built around one job in your business. It has a written job description, examples of good work, and a manager: you. It knows what it can do on its own and what it brings to you for approval.

Every digital employee is built around your business, so there’s no price list.

We run our own portfolio this way

One of ours reads every invoice, receipt and statement that arrives in our accounting inbox, then names it, files it and labels it.

Another keeps track of every bill across our properties and prepares a daily payables report for Colin.

They have taken real hours off our week, and we’ve learned what it takes to make them trustworthy.

Perhaps one of these is the place to start

01The spreadsheet you approach cautiously

Your property numbers live in a workbook you built years ago. One formula looks suspicious, and you’re hoping it has always looked that way.

02The partner report that takes your weekend

The information exists. Turning it into a clear update for your investment partners becomes a project of its own.

03The Canva rabbit hole

You open it to make one graphic. Forty-five minutes later, you’re comparing fonts and wondering whether you’ve ever liked blue.

04The investment update that looks homemade

You know your project inside out. Getting it into a clear, attractive one-pager is another skill entirely.

05The contractor quote comparison

Three quotes arrive in three formats. You need to see what’s included, what’s missing, and what to ask before deciding.

06The AI subscription waiting for a job

You’re paying for a tool and suspect it could help. Figuring out how to put it to work in your business is where you get stuck.

None of these? Tell us the task that eats an hour of your week. That’s usually the right first hire.

How we work with you

  1. Pick one job. Start with the task that eats an hour of your week.

  2. Walk us through it. We talk through how you do it now and agree on what a good result looks like.

  3. We build and test it with you. You see its work before it does anything on its own.

  4. You stay in charge. We agree what it handles alone, what needs your OK, and how you’ll check it.

  5. We measure it. Time saved, errors caught, work you no longer touch. Something concrete to review together.

Questions, answered

What is a digital employee?

An AI staff member built for one recurring job, with a written job description, a manager, and clear limits on what it does without approval.

Is it the same as a chatbot?

No. A chatbot answers questions. A digital employee does a recurring job, like filing invoices or preparing a report, and checks in with you.

Will it replace my staff?

It takes the repetitive part of a job so your people spend their time on the work that needs them.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the job. Book a call and we’ll scope your first one.

Is my information safe?

You decide what it can see and do. We start read-only where we can, and you approve anything that changes your files.
